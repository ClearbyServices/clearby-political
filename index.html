Responsible Political Communication & Advertising

Political communication requires attention to accuracy, transparency, permissions, and applicable regulations. Clearby Political aims to follow responsible practices while planning and executing digital and creative activities. Campaign advertising and communication must be assessed against the applicable Election Commission of India requirements, Model Code of Conduct, Media Certification and Monitoring Committee requirements where applicable, and advertising platform policies.

  • Using approved and verifiable information in campaign content.
  • Avoiding fabricated claims, misleading information, and false endorsements.
  • Following applicable political advertising disclosure and authorisation requirements.
  • Using contact information and personal data only with appropriate permissions and lawful grounds.
  • Respecting platform policies and applicable communication regulations.
  • Obtaining client approval before publishing campaign-related content.
